## Endeavors and Interests

I study computer science at TU Darmstadt and have been here since winter 2025. My aim is a research-oriented career in machine learning, though I am still figuring out exactly which problems deserve the most attention. I want to understand ML on both a theoretical and intuitive level, which means reading papers, implementing ideas, and occasionally discovering why something that sounded elegant falls apart in practice. Lately I have been experimenting with local learning and alternatives to backpropagation, including Hebbian approaches and variants of the forward-forward algorithm.

## Previous Projects

I have worked on a few things that taught me something. [Clac](/work/) is a programming language backend built around pattern matching and macro expansion. [LetoReader](/work/) is a speed reader I built because the existing ones felt overpriced for what they did. I also wrote a [research paper](/files/PV_Analyse_Facharbeit.pdf) on forecasting photovoltaic growth in Germany, which was my first real encounter with time series modeling and the gap between a decent model and a useful forecast. [ContextFlow](/work/) is a .NET library for structured LLM interactions that I built to make prompt chaining less ad-hoc. See [all work](/work/) for more.

## About Me

I am a night owl, which is either a productive trait or an excuse depending on the deadline. I listen to synthwave and phonk, go on long walks when I am stuck on a problem, and get genuinely excited by ideas that change how a field operates rather than marginally improving what already exists. Philosophically I lean toward anti-essentialism, naturalism, and structuralism (with bundle theory as a useful framing). In practice this means I want to get things done and be maximally pragmatic, but I also want to experiment and do creative things even when their purpose is not immediately clear.
