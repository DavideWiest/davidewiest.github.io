Mosaic is a link-as-bio platform adapted for projects rather than people. Users create accounts, add projects with descriptions and links, and share a single URL that displays everything. I worked on this in a team of two and focused primarily on the backend architecture and database design. It was built with PHP, Smarty templating, MySQL, and TailwindCSS.
